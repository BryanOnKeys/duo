# Community metrics

## Rules

- Write down the period and the source of every number. Repository traffic and clone counts are not unique users, and CI or bots inflate clones. npm downloads are not installs or continued use.
- Count people outside the six-person core team and bots separately.
- Write `N/A` for a number you cannot access, never 0.
- Keep aggregate numbers only. No private repositories, secrets, IP addresses or e-mail addresses.
- A star is a discovery signal. Verified first runs, external feedback and repeat contributions are the adoption signals.

## Recording

- When someone reports a first run, ask whether they finished setup, understood the limits and want to keep using DUO, and record the answer as they gave it.
- Keep core-team reports, independent volunteers and automated jobs apart.
- Feedback from casual users is not a study result. External Validation 01 and the proposed External Validation 02 have their own protocols.
